# Simple Barrels

Barrels that hold lots of a single item. Right click to put items in, left click to take a stack out, sneak left click for one.

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge and Fabric.

Also adds barrels for Biomes O' Plenty, Regions Unexplored and Nature's Spirit woods when those mods are installed.

Build with `./gradlew build`. Barrel json files are made by `python3 tools/gen_resources.py`.
